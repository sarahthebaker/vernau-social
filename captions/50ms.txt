Blink and you missed it. That flash was about how long people need to decide whether they like the look of a website.

In a 2006 study, people judged a web page's visual appeal after seeing it for just 50 milliseconds, and their snap judgements matched the ones they made with more time. It happens before they've read a single word about you.

So the first screen of your website isn't decoration. It's the first thing a potential client judges you on. Make that look count.

Source: Lindgaard et al. (2006), Behaviour & Information Technology 25(2).
