Selling online in Switzerland? Your website has to say who you are.

Under the Swiss Unfair Competition Act (UWG Art. 3 Abs. 1 lit. s), anyone offering goods, works or services online must give clear and complete details of their identity and their contact address, including an email address.

In practice that means an Impressum with your name, your address and your email that people can actually find. Not missing, not half-filled, not hidden behind a contact form.

General information, not legal advice. Source: fedlex.admin.ch.
