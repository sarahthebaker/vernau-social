Where do people actually look on a web page? Mostly right at the top.

In Nielsen Norman Group's eye-tracking research, people spent about 57% of their viewing time on the first screen of a page and 17% on the second. Everything below that shared the remaining 26%.

So the top of your homepage has one job: say what you do, who it's for, where you are and how to book, before anyone has to scroll.

Does yours?

Source: Nielsen Norman Group, "Scrolling and Attention" (2018, desktop eye-tracking study).
