Count to three. That's how long many people give a website on their phone.

Google's research found that 53% of mobile site visits are abandoned when a page takes longer than three seconds to load. It's an older study (2017), but the point has only become sharper as more people search on their phones.

The good news: speed is measurable, and most of what slows a site down can be fixed.
